Privacy Policy

Chunks

Last updated: 18 September 2026

Chunks collects nothing. Everything you type stays on your device. The app has no accounts, makes no network requests, and sends no information to us or to anyone else.

What the app stores

Chunks saves the following in your device's own private app storage:

  • Your classes and their colours
  • Your assignments — title, notes, due date and estimated length
  • A record of work you have ticked off
  • Your settings, including how much time you set aside each day and your reminder times

This data never leaves your device. It is not uploaded, backed up to us, or shared. If your device backs itself up through iCloud or a computer, that backup is controlled entirely by you and by Apple, not by us.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts. There is nothing to sign up for and no password to create.
  • No network requests. The app works fully offline and does not contact any server, including ours.
  • No analytics or tracking. No usage statistics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers, no third-party tracking SDKs.
  • No advertising. There are no ads and no ad networks.
  • No data sales. We have no data to sell, and would not sell it if we did.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally by your device. Nothing is sent through a push notification server, and the contents of your reminders — which is to say, your homework — never travel anywhere. If you decline notification permission, the rest of the app works normally.

Children's privacy

Chunks is designed for students, including those under 13. Because the app collects no personal information at all, there is nothing for a parent to request, review or have deleted from us. We hold no records about any user. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Deleting your data

Use Erase everything in Settings to clear all assignments, classes and settings, or delete the app. In both cases the data is gone from your device and, since we never had a copy, gone entirely.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Chunks ever collects anything, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be described here rather than buried.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised at github.com/shankaranandh/hw-manager/issues.
