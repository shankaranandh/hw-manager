Chunks

Homework, paced not crammed.

A due date tells you nothing useful on a Tuesday night. Chunks turns your assignments into tonight's list — a few short blocks, sized to the time you actually have.

What it does

  • Capture in seconds. One line of text, a class, a due day, and how big it feels. Sizes, not minutes, because nobody can estimate minutes.
  • Paces it for you. Work is spread across the evenings before it is due, earliest deadline first, onto your least busy nights. Big assignments finish a day early.
  • Says so when it will not fit. If the week is genuinely too full, you find out the day you add it — not the night before.
  • Two reminders a night, at most. One with the plan, one gentle check-in, and silence on a free evening.

Private by design

No accounts. No network requests. No analytics, no ads, no tracking. Everything you type stays on your device. Read the privacy policy — it is short, because there is very little to say.
