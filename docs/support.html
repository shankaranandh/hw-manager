Get in touch

The fastest way to report a problem or ask for something is to open an issue: github.com/shankaranandh/hw-manager/issues. Say what you expected, what happened instead, and which device you are on.

Common questions

Where did my reminders go?

Check three things, in order. Notifications must be allowed for Chunks in your device's Settings. Send me reminders must be on in the app's own Settings tab. And there must be something to remind you about — Chunks stays quiet on an evening with nothing planned, which is deliberate.

How far ahead are reminders scheduled?

Seven days. They are rewritten every time you change something, so opening the app at least once a week keeps them flowing. In normal use, adding assignments does this for you.

Why did it put my assignment on days I did not choose?

That is the point of the app. You give it a due date and a rough size, and it spreads the work across the evenings before, fitting it around the time you said you had. Change how much time you have on each day under Time you'll actually give it in Settings, and the plan rebuilds.

It says something will not fit. What now?

It means there are not enough free minutes before the due date to finish at the pace you set. You can raise the time you set aside for the days in between, cut the estimate if the assignment is smaller than you thought, or take the hint and ask for an extension. Chunks shows the work anyway rather than hiding it.

Can I move a block to a different day?

Not directly. The plan is rebuilt from your due dates and your available time every time anything changes, so the way to move work is to change one of those. This keeps the plan honest: it can never show you a schedule that does not add up.

I ticked something off by mistake.

Tick it again to undo. If you used Mark the whole thing finished, open the assignment and choose Reopen it.

Is my homework private?

Yes. Everything stays on your device and nothing is ever uploaded. See the privacy policy.

Can I move my data to a new phone?

Not yet. Data lives only on the device it was entered on, so a new phone starts empty. A standard device-to-device transfer or an encrypted backup restore will usually bring it across; a fresh install will not.
